# Style bible — DRAFT, waiting for owner approval

## Recurring character: Dave
- Ordinary modern man, early 30s, slightly chubby, messy brown hair, short stubble, big expressive eyes.
- Outfit (never changes, it is his silhouette): mustard-yellow hoodie, blue jeans, white sneakers, smartphone in hand.
- Personality: curious, overconfident, clueless about survival; always tries a modern solution first and it fails.
- Running gags: phone shows "No signal" in every era; he keeps losing a sneaker; a "Days survived" counter in the corner.
- Role in the story: the viewer's stand-in. The narrator explains, Dave suffers the consequences.
- Reference image: `studio/assets/dave-model-sheet.png` (always pass it as reference when generating new scenes).

## Narrator
- Calm, dry, slightly ironic male or female voice in English (US). One voice for every episode.
- 150–165 words per minute.

## Visual style
- Flat 2D vector cartoon, thick dark-brown outlines, no gradients, simple shapes (cut-out animation friendly).
- Palette: mustard `#E3A72F`, terracotta `#C4623A`, olive `#6B7A3A`, sand `#EAD9B8`, deep blue `#1F3A5F`, outline `#3B2A1E`.
- Deliberately NOT stick figures (Depth Theory / StickOrbit / Squire Explains own that look).
- Each era has its own background palette tint (Ice Age = cold blue, Egypt = sand, Medieval = olive/grey), Dave always keeps his colours so he pops out.

## Thumbnails
- Dave's face, big, with one strong expression (terrified / disgusted) on the right third.
- One era object or threat on the left (mammoth, plague doctor, pyramid).
- Max 3 words of text, thick white letters with dark outline. Often a number ("DAY 1", "3 HOURS?").
- Same layout every time so the channel is recognisable in the feed.
