## What we keep (patterns)
- Cold open with "you" + the air itself as threat.
- Clear ground rules early.
- Concrete survival estimate per era.
- Cliffhanger at every transition.
- Myth-busting beats.
- A twist ending line.

## What we do differently (so we are not a copycat)
- **Dave** experiences each era on screen; narrator explains, Dave suffers.
- **Countdown worst → best** (#12 → #1) instead of chronological: the open loop "which era is #1?" holds retention to the end.
- On-screen **survival timer** per era + a growing tier board.
- Running gags (no signal, lost sneaker).
- Shorter: target 11–13 min.
