Fix number three: a house. Dave stacks snow into a wall, very proud of his igloo. The wind removes it in about a minute. At Mezhyrich, people built round dwellings out of mammoth bones, about twenty to thirty feet across, with several hundred bones and tusks each, and a hearth in the middle. Hides most likely covered the frame. A 2025 dating study puts the largest structure at about eighteen thousand years ago, in the harshest part of the Ice Age. The same study suggests people used them briefly, so think winter cabin, not forever home. Not bad for a house made of leftovers. Inside there's a fire, a bed of furs, and no wind.
