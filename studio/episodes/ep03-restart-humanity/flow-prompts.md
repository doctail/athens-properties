Status: v2 (2026-11-15). Replaces v1 (29 images, too few: one image every ~16 s). Pace rule: one image every 5–7 s, each tied to the narrator line of its section, like EP02 (80 images for 7:46). Owner runs Flow. Do not generate without the owner's go.
Attach the Dave model sheet as reference in EVERY run. Download in order. Name files with the 3-digit number.
Runs 1–4: 16:9, 20 images each (001–020, 021–040, 041–060, 061–080). Runs 5–7: 9:16, 10 images each (Shorts S1, S2, S3: V01–V10, V11–V20, V21–V30).

## HEADER (paste at the top of every run)
```
Generate one separate image for each numbered prompt below, in the same order. Name each file with its number.
STYLE FOR ALL IMAGES: flat 2D vector cartoon, thick dark-brown outlines, no gradients, simple clean shapes, muted post-apocalyptic palette (grey sand, cold blue sea, faded sand, dusty terracotta accents, one warm mustard light source at camp), 16:9 landscape unless the prompt says 9:16. NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO readable signs, NO logos, NO watermarks.
SETTING: a grey empty beach after a world-wide catastrophe (never explained). In the far background a ruined city skyline, overgrown and empty. A small camp of two tents, a driftwood fence, a fire pit, one satellite dish leaning on a pole.
DAVE = the character in the reference image: man in his early 30s, slightly chubby, messy brown hair, short stubble, big expressive eyes, mustard-yellow hoodie, blue jeans, white sneakers, smartphone in hand (screen dark). Keep Dave IDENTICAL in every image, ONE Dave per image unless the prompt says otherwise.
CARLA = a woman in her 30s, dark hair tied back in a low ponytail, dark green utility jacket over a plain grey shirt, khaki cargo trousers, sturdy brown boots. No logos, no badges. Keep her identical in every image.
```
