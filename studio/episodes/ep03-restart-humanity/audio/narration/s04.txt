Plan B is the oldest plan in history: find more people. Carla draws a map in the sand, with arrows to far coastlines. She packs rope and water. Dave has his own plan: fix the satellite dish. It's one wire away from working. It is always one wire away from working. So Carla walks. Days later, she sees smoke on the horizon. Two strangers, thin and sick. Dave takes a step back. Finding more people is the only real way out of the trap. But every new person also brings new risks: disease, hunger, and one more mouth to feed.
