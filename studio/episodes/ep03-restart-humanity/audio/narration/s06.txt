So, could two people restart humanity? On their own, almost certainly not. Two people can build a shelter, catch fish and survive a winter. But they can't keep a species going. That takes a chain, and a chain needs more than two links. The genetics, the workload and the bad luck, one bad winter, one broken ankle, one infection, can end it at any time. What actually works is a group: dozens of people from different families, with enough hands to share the work, and generations to build on. That's how humans survived for hundreds of thousands of years. Final verdict: probably not, but nice try. And Dave would like you to know he is still not wearing a coat.
