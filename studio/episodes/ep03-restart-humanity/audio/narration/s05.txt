Skip ahead twenty years. The seasons keep turning, and against all odds there's a stone house, a garden, and smoke from a chimney. Dave and Carla found three more people along the way. They also found a handful of surviving seeds, and a lot of stubbornness. And now, there are children. Dave holds a baby. Carla says the baby has her nose. Dave shrugs. Every child needs food, shelter, and someone to pick them up when they fall. Dave becomes the group's reluctant teacher: one blank board, one sock puppet. The puppet bites him. The biggest threat now isn't hunger or weather. It's a single infection with nobody to treat it. Dave learns to boil water. Carla searches a burned-out library and finds a damp first aid book. She sets a broken arm from it. The first page is missing.
