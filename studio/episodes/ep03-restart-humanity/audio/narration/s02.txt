Forget babies for a moment. Year one is about staying alive. Dave tries to spear a fish, and misses. He tries a hand line and finally catches one. Then a seagull takes it. Carla builds a fence. She also has to watch the horizon. And that's the real problem: two adults can't hunt, cook, build, farm and keep watch at the same time. Hunter-gatherer groups that survived for thousands of years were usually dozens of people, because they shared the work. Dave's big idea is farming. It sounds great, but a harvest takes a whole season, and one bad winter can wipe it out. Carla's plan is simpler: eat what lives in the sea. Carla is right. Dave is not. But Carla is also one broken ankle away from disaster, and so is Dave. With nobody else to help, one injury could end the whole project.
