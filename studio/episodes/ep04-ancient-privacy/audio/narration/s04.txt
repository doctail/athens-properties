Fix number three: sleep. Surely night is private? Not really. Researchers tracked the sleep of Hadza adults for twenty days and found that everyone was asleep at the same moment for just eighteen minutes in total. Someone was nearly always awake. The authors suggest that people of different ages sleeping on different schedules worked like a built-in night watch. So at night, privacy lost to safety. Dave sleeps in a room with a built-in security system, and the security system is grandpa.
