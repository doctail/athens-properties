Then everything changed. As people settled down, walls got serious. At Çatalhöyük in Turkey, around nine thousand years ago, houses were packed together with no streets, and you entered by ladder through the roof. The rooftops were the streets, and the front door was a hole in the ceiling. And at Skara Brae in Scotland, about five thousand years ago, stone houses had doors that could be barred from the inside. Dave finally gets his lock. He's alone, in the dark, and thirty seconds later he's lonely.
