So, how did ancient humans get any privacy? Not with doors. They mixed a little wall with a lot of distance, manners, and the freedom to walk away. Wall, distance, night watch, manners, exit. That's our best reading of the evidence, not a certainty. Last time we said privacy was never a thing in the Stone Age. That wasn't quite fair. It was a thing. It just didn't have a door. Privacy wasn't a room. It was a skill.
