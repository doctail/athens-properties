Fix number two: walk away. Draper noted that foragers lived at very low population densities overall. So even a packed camp was surrounded by empty land. A few minutes' walk and you were out of sight. That is an inference from how foragers live today, but it is a simple one: privacy is easy to find when there are few people and a lot of space. It is probably also where most of life's less glamorous business happened. Dave is alone for exactly four minutes. Then it gets dark, and something far away is watching him back. Distance gave privacy, and it also took away safety. So Dave runs back to the group.
