## HEADER (paste at the top of every run)
```
Generate one separate image for each numbered prompt below, in the same order. Name each file with its number.
STYLE FOR ALL IMAGES: flat 2D vector cartoon, thick dark-brown outlines, no gradients, simple clean shapes, warm earthy palette (mustard yellow, terracotta, olive green, sand, deep blue), 16:9 landscape unless the prompt says 9:16. NO text, NO letters, NO hieroglyphs that look like writing, NO numbers, NO watermarks.
SETTING: Old Kingdom Egypt around 2560 BC, Giza, during the building of the Great Pyramid. Ordinary Egyptians wear simple white linen kilts or plain linen dresses, short dark hair or wigs, bare feet or reed sandals, black kohl eyeliner on men and women. NO pharaoh headdresses on ordinary people, NO camels, NO modern objects (no metal tools, glass, plastic, buildings with windows panes) except Dave's own clothes and smartphone. The Great Pyramid is UNFINISHED (stepped core, ramps around it) in every image where it appears.
DAVE = the character in the reference image: man in his early 30s, slightly chubby, messy brown hair, short stubble, big expressive eyes, mustard-yellow hoodie, blue jeans, white sneakers. Keep Dave IDENTICAL in every image, ONE Dave per image.
```
