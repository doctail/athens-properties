Real 3D city timelapses built year by year from official building data. No AI imagery. Just the city, the years and the music.

Ledger of Cities renders every city in 3D from open data: the real footprint, height and construction year of each building, placed in its real year and layered over historical maps of how the city looked before. Where a building's year is unknown or estimated, the video says so on screen.

New cities on a regular schedule. Tell us in the comments which city should be next.

Data sources: municipal and national open-data portals and cadastres, U.S. Census TIGER/Line, © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Each video lists its exact datasets and licenses in its description.

Rendered in Blender.
